PRIVACY POLICY

Organization: Emes Games  |  Product: WSJ (Winter Ski Jumping)  |  Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Emes Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling user-account and gameplay information lawfully, transparently, and respectfully. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our website (emesgames.party) and our game WSJ (Winter Ski Jumping), how that information is used and shared, how long it is retained, and how you can access or delete your data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Epic Account Services & Epic Online Services (EOS) Data: When you use online multiplayer features in WSJ, the game connects through Epic Online Services. Depending on your sign-in method, this may process your Epic Account ID, Product User ID (PUID), Epic display name, and temporary session/lobby metadata required to authenticate you and connect you with other players.
  • Network & Peer-to-Peer Relay Data: During online multiplayer matches, technical network information (such as relay routing metadata and gameplay state packets) is processed via Epic Online Services P2P relays to deliver real-time multiplayer sessions.
  • Local Game Data: Your single-player profiles, custom hills, ski jumper rosters, settings, and jump replays are stored locally on your own device. We do not upload your local saves to any Emes Games server.
  • Website Data: This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. Emes Games does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or behavioral profiling on this website. Standard web server logs may be processed by GitHub Pages in accordance with GitHub's privacy practices.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the limited information described above solely to:

  • Authenticate players for online multiplayer sessions via Epic Online Services.
  • Display your player name in multiplayer lobbies and competition standings.
  • Create, discover, join, and relay real-time multiplayer ski jumping sessions.
  • Respond to support and data-protection inquiries you send to us directly.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

3. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

We only share information with service providers strictly necessary to operate multiplayer and hosting functionality:

  • Epic Games, Inc. (Epic Online Services / Epic Account Services): For player authentication, session management, and P2P multiplayer relays. Epic Games processes this data under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages): For hosting this static website.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by applicable law or valid legal process.

4. DATA RETENTION

  • Emes Games Servers: Emes Games does not operate a proprietary backend database storing user accounts or personal profiles.
  • Multiplayer Sessions: Lobby and P2P session data is ephemeral and exists only for the duration of an active multiplayer match.
  • Local Device Files: Profiles, settings, and replays remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the game.
  • Support Emails: If you contact us by email, we retain your message only as long as needed to resolve your request and comply with legal obligations.
  • Epic Account Services Data: Retained by Epic Games according to your Epic Games account settings and Epic Games' retention policies.

5. YOUR RIGHTS: ACCESS AND DELETION

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your personal data:

  • Revoke Epic Account Access: You can revoke WSJ's access to your Epic Games account at any time in your Epic Games Account Connections settings.
  • Delete Local Data: You can delete your local profiles, custom hills, and replays directly from your device's game data folder at any time.
  • Contact Us for Data Requests: To request access to or deletion of any personal data associated with Emes Games or WSJ, email us at michal.sabiniarz@gmail.com. We will respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WSJ (Winter Ski Jumping) is a family-friendly winter sports game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent as required by applicable law (such as COPPA or GDPR-K). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as WSJ evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
